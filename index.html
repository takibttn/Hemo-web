About HemoCare

HemoCare is a revolutionary Android application designed to connect blood donors with recipients in real-time. Our mission is to make blood donation simpler, more efficient, and more rewarding for everyone involved.

With HemoCare, you can easily find nearby blood donation camps, track your donation history, receive alerts when your blood type is needed, and earn rewards for your life-saving contributions.

Secure and privacy-focused

Key Features

Emergency Alerts

Get instant notifications when your blood type is urgently needed nearby.

Donation Camp Locator

Find the nearest blood donation centers and mobile blood drives.

Donation Tracker

Keep track of your donation history and eligibility dates.

Reward System

Earn badges and recognition for your regular donations.

Getting Started

  1. Download and install HemoCare from Google Play Store
  2. Create your account with basic information
  3. Set your blood type and donation preferences
  4. Enable notifications to receive emergency alerts
  5. Start exploring donation opportunities near you
